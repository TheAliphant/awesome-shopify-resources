# A usable scope and pricing checklist for freelance Shopify projects

A small Shopify storefront brief can quietly turn into a full redesign, product import, content job, integration project and training package. Agree on **what is included** before quoting a fixed price. This checklist is independent editorial guidance for freelancers and studios; it is **not** a Shopify integration or official Shopify advice.

## 1. Define the outcome in one sentence

Example: “Design and configure a five-page storefront using the merchant's existing Shopify subscription and supplied product data.” Do not write only “build a Shopify website.”

Ask who supplies the brand assets, text, photography, product descriptions, policy copy, access and final approval.

## 2. Count deliverables, not vague features

| Workstream | Example included deliverable | Decision to make |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | One 60-minute kickoff and written scope | Is additional research charged separately? |
| Design | Home, collection, product and information-page layout | How many distinct templates and responsive states? |
| Content | Placement of client-supplied copy and imagery | Are writing, retouching and translation excluded? |
| Catalogue | Import of up to 25 supplied SKUs | Who corrects invalid product data? |
| Integrations | Standard payment, shipping and analytics setup | Are custom apps, paid subscriptions and API development excluded? |
| Review | Two consolidated revision rounds | What counts as a new request? |
| Handover | Published agreed pages, walkthrough and short checklist | What post-launch support, if any, is included? |

Only include rows you can actually deliver. Shopify plan fees, apps, paid themes, stock imagery, taxes and other third-party costs should have a named responsible payer and must never be silently absorbed.

## 3. Make exclusions as visible as inclusions

Examples: new brand identity, product photography, multilingual content, custom checkout coding, third-party app subscriptions, complex migration, copywriting, SEO guarantees, ongoing maintenance and unapproved additional rounds are **not included unless explicitly priced**.

State that changes outside the agreed scope require a separate written estimate before work starts.

## 4. Separate estimate, price and effective hourly rate

Illustration only (not a recommended market rate):

- Fixed project fee: **NOK 20,000 excluding VAT**
- Planned effort: **20 hours** → effective gross fee **NOK 1,000/hour**
- Actual effort after scope creep: **35 hours** → effective gross fee **about NOK 571/hour**

Neither figure is profit. Deduct subcontracting, software, taxes and other relevant costs when assessing whether the project makes financial sense. If you choose a fixed fee, decide who absorbs changes and unforeseen work.

## 5. Write acceptance and payment triggers

Specify what must be delivered for approval, where feedback arrives, how long the client has to respond, who has publish authority, when a change request becomes chargeable, and when each invoice is due. Verify the applicable consumer/business law and tax rules for your jurisdiction.

## 6. Copy this short offer outline

1. **Objective:** The business outcome.
2. **Included deliverables:** Exact pages, assets, integrations and limits.
3. **Client provides:** Copy, files, access, approvals and named contact.
4. **Excluded:** Unpriced work and third-party fees.
5. **Timeline and dependencies:** Milestones assuming timely inputs.
6. **Revisions:** Number of consolidated rounds and how extras are approved.
7. **Price and payment:** Currency, VAT treatment, deposit and final milestone.
8. **Acceptance and next step:** One explicit decision and contact route.
